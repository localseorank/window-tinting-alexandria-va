Virginia Window Tint Regulations and Compliance

Staying compliant with Virginia state tint laws is essential to passing vehicle safety inspections. Operating with tint that is too dark can result in vehicle inspection failures and municipal citations.

In Virginia, passenger vehicles are permitted 50% Visible Light Transmission (VLT) on front side windows and 35% VLT on rear side windows and the back windshield. Our experienced technicians install films that meet all legal VLT requirements while maximizing heat rejection.

Virginia legal tint guidelines:

  • ✓Front Side Windows: Must allow more than 50% light transmission
  • ✓Rear Side Windows and Back Glass: Must allow more than 35% light transmission
  • ✓Windshield: Non-reflective tint permitted exclusively above the AS-1 line

Our certified technicians check all completed installations with certified VLT meters, ensuring complete peace of mind during state safety inspections.
