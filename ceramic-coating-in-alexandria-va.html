The Technology Behind Liquid Ceramic Protection

Unlike conventional paste waxes that wash away after a few rains, ceramic coatings are liquid polymers formulated with pure silicon dioxide (SiO2) and titanium dioxide (TiO2). Getting ceramic coating in Alexandria, VA chemically bonds these advanced formulations to paint surfaces.

Upon curing, they chemically crosslink with the microscopic pores of your paint, creating an ultra-dense, glass-hard protective layer that resists environmental chemicals and oxidation. This permanent covalent bond cannot be stripped by pressure washing or detergents.

Key technological characteristics:

  • ✓High-density SiO2 quartz matrix providing hardness and physical scratch resistance
  • ✓Covalent molecular bonding that forms an inseparable layer atop factory clear coats
  • ✓Hydrophobic surface chemistry yielding contact angles greater than 110 degrees

By applying premier ceramic coating in Alexandria, VA directly to clear coat lacquers, Capital Wrappers provides lasting protection and incredible optical depth.

Extreme Water Beading and Self-Cleaning Hydrophobics

The ultra-low surface energy of cured ceramic coatings creates a high water contact angle exceeding 110 degrees. Water, mud, and road grime cannot bond to the surface; instead, moisture beads tightly and rolls away, taking surface dirt along with it.

Uncoated paint absorbs moisture and road grime into microscopic clear coat pores, requiring frequent, aggressive washing. Ceramic-coated surfaces actively repel moisture and contaminants.

Hydrophobic surface benefits:

  • ✓Water contact angles over 110 degrees for rapid water beading and runoff
  • ✓Self-cleaning performance that sheds dust and road grime during rainy weather
  • ✓Faster and safer hand washing requiring gentle soap and light agitation

Your car stays visibly cleaner for weeks and washes effortlessly, saving you valuable time and detailing effort.

Maintenance Guidelines for Lasting Gloss

Maintaining a ceramic-coated vehicle is simple and rewarding. We recommend hand washing with the two-bucket technique, utilizing pH-balanced automotive shampoo, and drying with plush microfiber drying towels.

Avoid abrasive automated car washes that brush grit across panels and inflict micro-scratches. Following our simple aftercare routine ensures your coating maintains peak hydrophobicity and mirror reflections.

Recommended aftercare routine:

  1. 1Pre-rinse the vehicle with clean water to flush away loose grit and surface dust
  2. 2Hand-wash panels using the two-bucket method and pH-neutral automotive shampoo
  3. 3Dry with clean, high-pile microfiber drying towels using gentle blotting motions
  4. 4Apply a ceramic maintenance booster spray every 3 to 4 months to replenish hydrophobic properties

Following these basic maintenance steps keeps your ceramic coating performing at its highest level for years.
